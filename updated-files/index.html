About Us

A hidden tropical paradise

Nestled in the breathtaking Mergui Archipelago of Southern Myanmar, Cavern Island Resort (Hlaing Gu Island) is a hidden tropical paradise surrounded by pristine beaches, crystal clear waters, lush tropical forests, and untouched natural beauty.

From thrilling ocean adventures to peaceful moments by the sea, Cavern Island Resort brings together nature, adventure, and relaxation for an unforgettable island escape.

Getting Here

How to get to Cavern Island

Three easy legs from Yangon to our private beach. The last one is a scenic speedboat ride through the Mergui Archipelago.

  1. By air
    01

    Yangon → Kawthaung Airport

    Take a domestic flight from Yangon to Kawthaung Airport.

  2. By road
    02

    Kawthaung Airport → Main Jetty

    Take a bus from Kawthaung Airport to Kawthaung Main Jetty (မြို့မဆိပ်ကမ်း).

  3. By sea · 1h 40m
    03

    Main Jetty → Cavern Island

    Take a speedboat from Kawthaung Main Jetty to Cavern Island. The journey takes approx. 1 hour 40 minutes.

Tell us your flight and we'll arrange your boat transfer from Kawthaung.
